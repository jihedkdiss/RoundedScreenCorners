Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Rounded Screen Corners ("the app") is a desktop utility for Windows that adds customizable rounded corner overlays to your displays. This document describes what data the app collects, stores, and transmits.

Rounded Screen Corners does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests.

Data stored locally

Rounded Screen Corners stores the following information on your computer only. None of it leaves your device.

WhatWherePurpose
Settings (corner radius, rounding mode, monitor target, startup, tray, theme) %LOCALAPPDATA%\RoundedScreenCorners\settings.json Persisting your preferences between launches
Crash log %LOCALAPPDATA%\RoundedScreenCorners\crash.log Local diagnostics if the app crashes
Startup registration Microsoft Store StartupTask in packaged builds Optional "Launch at startup" feature

You can delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\RoundedScreenCorners\ at any time to remove all stored data.

Data transmitted

None. Rounded Screen Corners does not call out to the internet, does not include any analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting service, and does not contact any server controlled by the developer or any third party.

Microsoft Store

If you obtained Rounded Screen Corners via the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect installation and usage telemetry under their own privacy statement. That telemetry is collected by the Microsoft Store platform itself, not by Rounded Screen Corners.

Children

Rounded Screen Corners is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. The change history is also available in the app's repository commit log.
