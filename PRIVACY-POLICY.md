# Privacy Policy

> The canonical, hosted version of this policy lives at <https://jihedkdiss.github.io/RoundedScreenCorners/privacy.html>. This file is the source for that page.

**Last updated:** September 30, 2026

Rounded Screen Corners ("the app") is a desktop utility for Windows that adds configurable rounded corner overlays to your displays. This document describes what data the app collects, stores, and transmits.

## Summary

**Rounded Screen Corners does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** The app runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests.

## Data stored locally

Rounded Screen Corners stores the following information on your computer only. None of it leaves your device.

| What | Where | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| Settings | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RoundedScreenCorners\settings.json` | Persisting your display, corner, tray, startup, and theme preferences |
| Crash log | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RoundedScreenCorners\crash.log` | Local diagnostics if the app crashes |
| Startup registration | Microsoft Store StartupTask in packaged builds | Optional launch at startup feature |

You can delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RoundedScreenCorners\` at any time to remove all locally stored app data.

## Data transmitted

**None.** Rounded Screen Corners does not call out to the internet, does not include analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting services, and does not contact any server controlled by the developer or any third party.

## Microsoft Store

If you obtained Rounded Screen Corners through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect installation and usage telemetry under their own [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). That telemetry is collected by the Microsoft Store platform itself, not by Rounded Screen Corners.

## Children

Rounded Screen Corners is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new "Last updated" date.
